第一章 · 拐点：2004Ch.1 · The Turning Point: 2004

荒漠化，第一次"刹住了车"Desertification Hit the Brakes for the First Time

上世纪末，中国荒漠化土地每年仍在扩展。2004 年，全国荒漠化和沙化土地面积首次实现"双缩减"——这是历史性的拐点。At the end of the last century, China's desertified land was still expanding every year. In 2004, both desertified and sandy land shrank for the first time nationwide — a historic turning point.

第四章 · 治沙三法与先锋植物Ch.4 · Three Methods & Pioneer Plants

中国人治沙的智慧The Wisdom of China's Sand Fighters

🌾 生物治沙 —— 以绿治荒Biological control — greening the barren

乔灌草结合，封沙育林育草。在流动沙丘迎风坡扎设沙障、背风坡种灌木，让沙漠"穿上绿衣"。Trees, shrubs and grasses combined; sand fenced off for forest and grass recovery. Barriers on windward slopes, shrubs on leeward slopes — dressing the desert in green.

🧱 工程治沙 —— 给沙漠"打格子"Engineering control — gridding the desert

草方格、沙障、封禁保护带。包兰铁路两侧的草方格固沙带，被称为"世界治沙奇迹"。Checkerboards, sand barriers, protected enclosures. The checkerboard belts along the Baotou–Lanzhou Railway are hailed as a "world miracle of sand control".

💧 综合治沙 —— 沙里淘金Integrated control — gold from the sand

光伏治沙板下种草、沙漠旅游、梭梭嫁接肉苁蓉——让治沙也能产生经济价值，形成良性循环。Grass under solar panels, desert tourism, cistanche grafted onto haloxylon — making sand control economically rewarding in a virtuous cycle.

🌵 先锋植物天团Pioneer Plant All-Stars

它们是第一批冲进沙漠的"绿色士兵"。They were the first "green soldiers" to charge into the desert.

🌿

梭梭Haloxylon

"沙漠植被之王"。根系深达 10 米，耐旱耐盐碱，还能嫁接名贵药材肉苁蓉。"King of desert vegetation." Roots reach 10 m deep; drought- and salt-tolerant; hosts prized medicinal cistanche.

🌾

沙柳Salix psammophila

"平茬复壮"越砍越旺，是固沙造林的先锋，也是优质的生物质能源。Thrives on coppicing — the vanguard of sand-fixing forests and quality biomass energy.

🌼

柠条Caragana

耐旱、耐寒、耐贫瘠，花开时一片金黄，还是优质的饲料和蜜源植物。Drought-, cold- and barren-tolerant; blooms golden; fine forage and honey plant.

🫐

沙棘Sea buckthorn

"维生素 C 之王"，果实营养丰富，根系固沙能力极强，兼具生态与经济价值。"King of vitamin C"; nutritious berries, powerful sand-fixing roots — ecological and economic value in one.
